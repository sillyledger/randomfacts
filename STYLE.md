# Style guide

Every fact (title, explain, story) and every piece of site copy follows these rules.
`npm run build` runs `scripts/check-style.ts`, which blocks the deploy on dashes and banned words.

## Punctuation

- No em dashes (—) or en dashes (–). Use a period or a comma instead.

## Voice

- Short sentences and plain words, the way you'd tell a friend.
- Don't open with a question.
- Don't end with a moral, a joke or a flourish. Stop when the fact is told.
- Match the tone of the existing cards.

## Banned words and phrases

Never use:

- fascinating
- remarkable
- incredible
- amazing
- delve
- testament
- intriguing
- boasts
- notably
- crucial
- it's worth noting
- in fact
- fun fact
- did you know

## Numbers

- Write numbers as digits with units: 3 mm, 225 days.

## Titles

- One short sentence ending in a period.
